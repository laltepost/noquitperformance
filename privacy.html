Privacy Policy.

How we collect, use, and protect your data. Updated March 2025.

001 / Overview

We collect the minimum. We protect what matters.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect from you when you visit noquitperformance.com, make a purchase, or interact with us, and how we use, share, and protect that information.

We're transparent. We don't sell your data. We collect only what we need to ship your order, send you updates, and improve your experience.

002 / What We Collect

Information You Provide

When you place an order or contact us, we collect:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number.
  • Shipping Address: Full address, city, state, ZIP code for delivery.
  • Payment Information: Credit card or payment method details. (Payment processing is handled securely by Shopify Payments; we do not store full card numbers.)
  • Order Details: Products purchased, quantities, order total.
  • Communications: Any messages you send us via email or contact form.
  • Subscription Information: If you subscribe to our product, we store your subscription preferences and renewal date via our subscription provider, Seal Subscriptions.

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, we automatically collect:

  • Browser Data: IP address, browser type, operating system, pages visited, time spent on site.
  • Cookies: We use cookies to remember your cart, preferences, and login status.
  • Tracking Pixels: We use Meta Pixel and Google Analytics to track user behavior and measure campaign performance.
  • Device Information: Device type, resolution, referring domain.

Note: We do not use third-party tracking services to identify you across other websites. Our tracking is limited to our own site and conversion measurement for advertising purposes.

003 / How We Use Your Data

Your data serves one purpose: your experience.

We use your information to:

  • Process Orders: Fulfill and ship your order, charge your payment method, and confirm your purchase.
  • Communication: Send order confirmations, shipping updates, and customer support responses via email.
  • Email Marketing: Send promotional emails, product updates, and content only if you've opted in. You can unsubscribe anytime.
  • Subscription Management: Manage recurring charges, renewals, and billing for Subscribe & Save orders.
  • Analytics & Improvement: Analyze how visitors use our site to improve design, navigation, and product offerings.
  • Advertising: Use conversion pixels (Meta, Google) to measure ad performance and retarget users who visited our site.
  • Fraud Prevention: Monitor for suspicious activity to protect your account and our business.
  • Legal Compliance: Comply with laws, regulations, and payment processor requirements (e.g., PCI-DSS for payment data).

We do not use your data to profile you or make automated decisions that significantly affect you.

004 / Third-Party Services

Partners we trust with your data.

We share your information only with service providers who process it on our behalf under strict confidentiality agreements. These partners include:

  • Shopify: Our e-commerce platform handles checkout, payment processing (via Shopify Payments), and order management. Shopify Privacy Policy
  • Seal Subscriptions: Our subscription platform manages recurring orders and billing. Seal Subscriptions Privacy Policy
  • Klaviyo: Our email service provider sends order confirmations and marketing emails. Klaviyo Privacy Policy
  • Pirate Ship: Our shipping and fulfillment partner prints labels and manages logistics. Pirate Ship Privacy Policy
  • Google Analytics: We track site traffic and user behavior. Google Privacy Policy
  • Meta Pixel: We measure conversions from Facebook and Instagram ads. Meta Privacy Policy

These partners are contractually bound to use your information only as necessary to provide their services. We do not authorize them to sell, rent, or transfer your data to other parties.

005 / Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies. You can control them.

Cookies are small files stored on your browser that remember your preferences. We use cookies for:

  • Essential Cookies: Session management, cart functionality, login persistence.
  • Analytics Cookies: Track page views, traffic sources, user behavior via Google Analytics.
  • Marketing Cookies: Meta Pixel tracks conversions and allows us to retarget you with ads on Facebook/Instagram.
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your site preferences (e.g., currency, language).

You can disable cookies in your browser settings. Disabling essential cookies may affect site functionality. Most browsers allow you to opt out of analytics and marketing cookies specifically.

006 / Data Retention

We keep what you need. We delete the rest.

  • Order Information: Retained for 7 years for tax and accounting purposes, as required by law.
  • Email List: Retained until you unsubscribe or request deletion.
  • Cookies & Analytics: Typically expire within 90 days or when you clear your browser.
  • Support Communications: Retained for 2 years or as needed for dispute resolution.
  • Deleted Accounts: Account data deleted upon request, except where required by law.

007 / Security

We protect your data like we protect our product.

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

  • SSL Encryption: All data transmitted to and from our site is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • PCI-DSS Compliance: Payment information is handled by Shopify Payments, which meets Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard requirements. We do not store credit card numbers.
  • Access Controls: Only authorized employees access customer data, and only as necessary.
  • Regular Audits: We monitor for vulnerabilities and suspicious activity.

While we take reasonable precautions, no online system is completely secure. If you believe your account is compromised, contact us immediately at hello@noquitperformance.com.

008 / California Privacy Rights

Your CCPA and CalOPPA Rights

If you are a California resident, you have the following rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Online Privacy Protection Act (CalOPPA):

  • Right to Know: You may request what personal information we collect and how we use it.
  • Right to Delete: You may request deletion of your personal information (with limited exceptions for legal/business purposes).
  • Right to Opt-Out: You may opt out of the "sale or sharing" of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. (We do not sell your data, but we do use pixels for retargeting ads, which may constitute "sharing" under CCPA.)
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

To exercise these rights, email hello@noquitperformance.com with "CCPA Request" in the subject line and include your name and the email address associated with your account. We will respond within 45 days. You may submit requests twice per calendar year.

You may also designate an authorized agent to submit requests on your behalf (with written authorization).

Do Not Track (CalOPPA)

Some browsers include a "Do Not Track" (DNT) feature. Currently, there is no industry standard for recognizing DNT signals, and we do not alter our tracking practices in response to DNT requests. You can control tracking through your cookie settings and browser privacy settings.

009 / Children & Minors

Not for kids.

Our site and products are not intended for individuals under 18 years old. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, we will promptly delete it. If you believe a child has submitted information to us, please contact hello@noquitperformance.com.

010 / Links & External Sites

We're not responsible for other sites.

Our site may contain links to third-party websites (e.g., research articles, partner sites). This Privacy Policy applies only to noquitperformance.com. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of linked sites. Always review the privacy policies of external websites before providing your information.
